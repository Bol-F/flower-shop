# Trivium • Picture and sound board

Ten minutes, 16:9, pure white. The recurring object is one bit with a fixed address: read it, follow it down a wire, then follow the same address after a clock tick. The register colors stay attached to their rows. On compact memory strips a filled cell is one, and an open cell is zero. Red underlines mark actual differences. Violet marks XOR, and green follows a bit that can leave the machine. Use the edge of a cell, the baseline of a byte or a wire endpoint to carry the eye from one construction into the next.

The shot names match the headings in the narration and the animation source. Speech is recorded in individual sentences. The beat boundaries in the cue sheet are taken from their recorded waveforms. The exact start times and subtitle cues ship in `data/timing.json`.
